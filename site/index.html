Our Story

About Us

Sanjay and Asha came to the United States in 2011 and, after many disappointing experiences with Indian vegetarian cuisine, wanted to bring the authentic flavors of Gujarati food to their community. They opened Rajwadi Thali, Chaat & Sweets to share the true taste of traditional Indian vegetarian cuisine through a carefully crafted thali experience.

Each thali combines a variety of dishes to deliver balanced nutrition, vibrant flavors, and textures that delight the senses. Their goal is to create a complete meal that satisfies sight, smell, sound, touch, and taste, offering an authentic and memorable Gujarati dining experience.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Thursday 11:00am–9:30pm, Friday and Saturday 11:00am–10:30pm, and Sunday 11:00am–10:00pm. Hours can change on public holidays — call (510) 896-8976 if you are planning a late visit.

What is an unlimited Gujarati thali?

A thali is a complete Gujarati meal served on one platter — several vegetable curries, dal, kadhi, rice, khichdi, freshly made roti and puri, plus a dessert. Ours is unlimited: every item is refilled at your table for as long as you are eating, so you taste the whole spread rather than choosing one dish.

How much does the unlimited thali cost?

Three thalis, all all-you-can-eat, all per person: the Rajwadi Silver Thali at $20.99, the Rajwadi Platinum Thali at $24.99, and the Rajwadi Executive Thali at $26.99. The Executive also includes complimentary unlimited chaats and desserts.

What is the difference between the Silver, Platinum and Executive thali?

The Silver Thali covers the everyday Gujarati meal — Khaman-Dhokla, chaas, aloo and vegetable curries, Surti dal and kadhi, khichdi, rice, roti and fresh AamRas. Platinum adds Gujarati Surti Undhiyu, Shahi Paneer, Kala Chana Masala, puri and dryfruit Srikhand. Executive is the Platinum spread plus appetizers and complimentary unlimited chaats and desserts.

Do you have Jain and vegan options?

Yes — every unlimited thali is available in a Jain preparation (no onion, no garlic, no root vegetables) and a vegan preparation. Tell your server when you sit down and the kitchen will plate accordingly.

Is all of your food vegetarian?

Our kitchen is 100% vegetarian and eggless. We never cook meat, poultry, fish or shellfish, and no dish on the menu contains egg. Full details are on our allergen and dietary information page.

Can two people share one unlimited thali?

No — sharing of the unlimited thali is not allowed, since each thali is priced per person for unlimited refills. Everyone at the table who is eating the thali orders their own. Our combos, chaats and kids thalis are à la carte and can be shared freely.

Can I take unlimited thali leftovers home?

The unlimited thali is dine-in only. TOGO is not permitted for it and boxes are not provided, which is also why we ask guests not to waste food — take a refill rather than a full plate you may not finish.

Do you offer takeout or delivery?

Yes, for everything except the unlimited thali. Combos such as Chole Bhature and Mumbai Pav Bhaji, all our Indian street chaats, kids thalis, drinks and sweets travel well. Order online or call (510) 896-8976.

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes. Two small-portion kids thalis at $15.99 each: the Rajwadi AamRas Puri Thali and the Rajwadi Srikhand Puri Thali, each with one vegetable curry of your choice — Undhiyu, Paneer or Aloo Matar — and three puri.

What are your most popular dishes?

The Rajwadi Executive Thali and Rajwadi Platinum Thali are what most guests come back for, followed by our Amritsari Chole Bhature at $17.99. Among the chaats, Pani Puri, Fresh Samosa Chaat and Dahi Sev Puri Chaat go out fastest.

Is there a service charge for large groups?

Yes — we add a 15% service charge for groups of five or more. Menu items are also subject to change based on daily availability.

Do you handle food allergies?

Tell your server before your first course arrives and we will flag the affected dishes. Ours is a shared kitchen with shared fryer oil, so we cannot guarantee any dish is free of trace allergens. Please read our allergen and dietary information before ordering if an allergy in your party is severe.

Where are you located?

3352 Mowry Ave, Fremont, CA 94538. We serve guests from across the Bay Area — Fremont, Newark, Union City, Milpitas, Hayward, Pleasanton and San Jose.

Do you cater parties and events?

Yes — Gujarati and North Indian catering for weddings, corporate events and family celebrations is handled by our catering arm at rajwadicatering.com, including live food stations and Jain menus.
